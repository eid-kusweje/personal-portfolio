HOW TO OPEN:
Double-click index.html to open it in any web browser.

HOW TO CUSTOMIZE:
1. Replace "Your Name" in index.html with your real name.
2. Add your photo to the images folder and name it profile.jpg.
3. Update your email, phone number, LinkedIn and GitHub links.
4. Replace the example projects with your own projects.
5. Change youremail@gmail.com in js/script.js.
6. Adjust skill percentages to match your level.
